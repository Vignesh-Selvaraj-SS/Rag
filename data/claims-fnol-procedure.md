# Claims Procedure CP-04 — First Notice of Loss and Claim Triage

**Document:** CP-04, revision 7
**Effective:** 15 February 2026
**Owner:** Claims Operations, Meridian Mutual
**Applies to:** All personal lines property claims, including endorsement pack claims

---

## 1. Intake channels

First Notice of Loss (FNOL) may be received through any of:

| Channel | Availability | Auto-acknowledgement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claims contact centre | 24 hours, every day | Verbal claim number at end of call |
| Policyholder web portal | 24 hours, every day | Email within 15 minutes |
| Mobile app | 24 hours, every day | Push notification, immediate |
| Agent or broker submission | Business hours | Email within 1 hour |
| Email to claims intake | 24 hours, every day | Email within 4 hours |

A **claim number must be issued within 30 minutes** of FNOL through every channel. A claim
is never held unnumbered pending coverage confirmation.

## 2. Mandatory FNOL data

The intake handler must capture:

1. Policy number and the name of the person reporting
2. Date, time and location of loss
3. Cause of loss, in the reporter's own words
4. Whether the dwelling is **habitable**, and whether emergency accommodation is needed
5. Whether any injury occurred
6. Whether emergency mitigation is already underway, and by whom
7. Photographs where the reporting channel supports upload

Where habitability is in doubt, the handler must treat the claim as **habitability at
risk** and apply the Tier 1 response below, without waiting for adjuster confirmation.

## 3. Severity tiers and response targets

| Tier | Definition | First contact target | Inspection target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 — Emergency | Dwelling uninhabitable, injury, active water or fire, or loss over $100,000 | **4 hours** | 24 hours |
| Tier 2 — Priority | Habitable but significant damage, or reserve $25,000–$100,000 | **1 business day** | 3 business days |
| Tier 3 — Standard | Contained damage, reserve under $25,000 | **2 business days** | 5 business days |
| Tier 4 — Desk | Contents-only, or documented estimate already supplied | **3 business days** | Not required |

Endorsement-specific overrides:

- **Water backup (HO-2026-01)** claims are Tier 2 minimum, with first contact within
  **one business day**.
- **Identity fraud (HO-2026-07)** claims route to the Specialty Lines desk with a
  **two business day** first contact target.
- **Equipment breakdown (HO-2026-03)** claims above $25,000 route to the specialist unit.
- **Service line (HO-2026-02)** claims target payment within **10 business days** of FNOL.

## 4. Emergency mitigation authority

Intake handlers may authorise emergency mitigation up to **$5,000** without adjuster
approval, for water extraction, board-up, tarping, or temporary heating. Above $5,000, a
field adjuster or the duty manager must approve.

Additional living expense may be advanced at intake up to **$3,000** where the dwelling is
uninhabitable, against the Coverage D limit.

## 5. Coverage confirmation

Coverage is confirmed by the adjuster, not at intake. Intake handlers must **not** tell a
policyholder that a loss is covered or excluded. The permitted wording is that the claim
will be reviewed against the policy and the policyholder will be contacted within the tier
target.

Where an endorsement is potentially relevant, the handler flags it on the claim record so
the adjuster reviews the correct form. The **endorsement schedule on the Declarations
page is authoritative** — the absence of an endorsement document in the claim file does not
mean the endorsement was not purchased.

## 6. Reopen and duplicate handling

A new FNOL for the same date of loss and cause within **90 days** is treated as a
**supplement** to the existing claim, not a new claim. After 90 days a new claim number is
issued and linked. Duplicate claim numbers issued in error must be closed with reason code
**DUP-01**, never deleted.

## 7. Escalation

The duty manager must be notified within one hour where the claim involves: a fatality, a
media enquiry, a suspected fraud indicator, a regulator contact, a total loss of the
dwelling, or any claim where the policyholder states they intend to instruct a lawyer.
