# Endorsement HO-2026-01 — Water Backup and Sump Discharge or Overflow

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** $68 annual, or $112 annual at the $25,000 limit option
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

Exclusion 3 of the base form excludes water which backs up through sewers or drains. This
endorsement **deletes that portion of the exclusion** and adds the coverage described
below. All other terms of exclusion 3 — including flood, surface water and overflow of a
body of water — remain fully excluded and are **not** restored by this endorsement.

## Coverage provided

Meridian Mutual will pay for direct physical loss to covered property caused by water, or
waterborne material, which:

1. **backs up through sewers or drains**; or
2. **overflows or is discharged from a sump, sump pump or related equipment**, even where
   the overflow results from mechanical breakdown of the sump pump.

Coverage applies to Coverage A, Coverage B and Coverage C property, and to Coverage D
loss of use arising from a covered water backup event.

## Limit of liability

The limit is **$10,000 per occurrence** unless the Declarations show the optional
**$25,000** limit. This limit is the most Meridian Mutual will pay in any one occurrence
regardless of the number of items damaged or the number of claimants.

The water backup limit is **not** an additional amount of insurance for Coverage A. Any
payment under this endorsement reduces the amount available under the applicable
Section I coverage for the same occurrence.

## Deductible

A **separate deductible of $500** applies to each water backup occurrence. This deductible
applies **instead of**, not in addition to, the base all-perils deductible of $1,000.

## Conditions

Coverage under this endorsement applies **only if**:

- the sump pump and any related equipment were in **proper working order** at the time of
  loss; and
- for dwellings with a finished basement, a **battery backup sump system or water alarm**
  is installed, where the dwelling was built or the basement finished after 01 January 2020.

Where the required backup system is absent, the limit is reduced to **$2,500** rather than
being voided entirely.

## Exclusions specific to this endorsement

This endorsement does not cover:

- loss caused by the insured's **failure to maintain** the sump pump, drain or sewer
  connection, where that failure is the sole cause of loss;
- **continuous or repeated seepage** over a period of more than 14 days, whether or not
  the insured was aware of it;
- loss resulting from **flood**, as defined in the base form, even where flood water
  subsequently enters through a drain or sump;
- **mould, fungus or wet rot** resulting from a water backup event, beyond the sub-limit
  in endorsement HO-2026-01A, if attached.

## Claim handling note

Water backup claims are assigned to the **Property — Water team** and carry a target
first-contact time of **one business day** from First Notice of Loss. A moisture-mapping
inspection is required before any payment above **$5,000**.
