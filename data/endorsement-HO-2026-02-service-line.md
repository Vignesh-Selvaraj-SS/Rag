# Endorsement HO-2026-02 — Service Line Coverage

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** $42 annual
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

Provides coverage for the cost to repair or replace a **damaged service line** running
between the dwelling and the utility connection point, together with the excavation and
restoration costs that repair requires. Service line failure is otherwise excluded under
the base form as wear and tear, deterioration or mechanical breakdown.

## Definition of service line

A **service line** means the underground piping or wiring on the residence premises that
provides a utility service to the dwelling, and which the insured is legally responsible to
maintain. This includes:

- water supply and sewer lines
- power, communication and data lines
- natural gas and steam lines
- geothermal and drainage piping

Service line coverage **begins at the point the line leaves the exterior wall of the
dwelling** and ends at the utility company's connection point or the boundary of the
residence premises, whichever comes first.

## Covered causes of loss

Coverage applies to damage caused by:

- wear and tear, marring, deterioration or latent defect
- rust, corrosion, decay or rot
- mechanical or electrical breakdown
- freezing or frost heave
- collapse of the line, or of earth surrounding the line
- **tree and shrub root intrusion**
- rodent, insect or vermin damage
- external force including excavation by the insured or a third party
- artificially generated electrical current

## Limit of liability

The limit is **$15,000 per occurrence**, which is the most payable for all costs combined,
including:

| Cost element | Included within the $15,000 limit |
| --- | --- |
| Repair or replacement of the service line | Yes |
| Excavation and backfill | Yes |
| Restoration of lawn, driveway and hardscape | Yes, capped at **$2,500** |
| Additional living expense while service is out | Yes, capped at **$1,500** |
| Loss of covered personal property caused by the failure | Yes |

## Deductible

A **$500 deductible** applies per service line occurrence, in place of the base all-perils
deductible.

## Exclusions specific to this endorsement

This endorsement does not cover:

- service lines **not connected to and serving the dwelling** — for example a line serving
  only a detached rental structure or a pool house operated as a business;
- lines the **utility company is responsible to maintain**;
- **septic tanks and leach fields** themselves, although the piping running to them is
  covered;
- loss caused by a service line failure that was **known to the insured before the policy
  inception**;
- costs to upgrade a line to meet a **current building code**, unless endorsement
  HO-2026-05 Ordinance or Law is also attached;
- damage to the **dwelling foundation** caused by long-term leakage where the leak
  persisted for more than 30 days before discovery.

## Claim handling note

Service line claims require a **licensed contractor's diagnostic report** identifying the
failure point before payment is authorised. Where the failure point falls on the utility
side of the connection, the claim is denied and referred to the utility for subrogation.
Target cycle time from First Notice of Loss to payment is **10 business days**.
