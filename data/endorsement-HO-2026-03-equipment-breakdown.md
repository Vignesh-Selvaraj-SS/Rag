# Endorsement HO-2026-03 — Home Equipment Breakdown

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** $55 annual
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

Exclusion 10 of the base form excludes mechanical breakdown, latent defect and damage
from artificially generated electrical current. This endorsement **adds back** coverage for
sudden and accidental **breakdown of covered equipment** at the residence premises.

## Definition of breakdown

**Breakdown** means the sudden and accidental physical damage to covered equipment that
requires its repair or replacement, arising from:

- mechanical breakdown, including rupture or bursting caused by centrifugal force
- artificially generated electrical current, including electrical arcing
- explosion of steam boilers, steam pipes or steam engines
- loss or damage to hot water heating systems from freezing
- **power surge originating off the residence premises**, including surge from a utility
  fault or a lightning strike to the grid

Breakdown does **not** mean wear and tear on its own, gradual deterioration, rust,
corrosion, or a defect that has caused no sudden physical damage.

## Covered equipment

Permanently installed or portable equipment at the residence premises, including:

- heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems, including heat pumps
- water heaters, well pumps and water treatment systems
- electrical service panels, wiring and standby generators
- **solar photovoltaic panels, inverters and battery storage systems**
- **electric vehicle supply equipment (home charging stations)**
- home appliances: refrigerators, freezers, ovens, dishwashers, washers, dryers
- home electronics: televisions, computers, home theatre and smart home controllers
- swimming pool and spa equipment, including pumps and heaters

## Equipment not covered

- any vehicle, watercraft or aircraft, including the **electric vehicle itself** — only the
  home charging station is covered
- equipment used primarily in a **business**, unless endorsement HO-2026-06 is attached
- equipment still covered by a **manufacturer's warranty or a service contract**, to the
  extent that warranty responds
- water piping, sanitary piping and sprinkler systems
- equipment more than **25 years old** where replacement parts are no longer manufactured

## Limit of liability

The limit is **$100,000 per breakdown occurrence**. The following additional coverages
apply **within** that limit:

| Additional coverage | Sub-limit |
| --- | --- |
| Spoilage of refrigerated food and medication | $1,000 |
| Expediting expenses to speed up repair | $5,000 |
| Environmentally friendly ("green") replacement upgrade | $5,000 |
| Increased cost to comply with current code | $10,000 |
| Loss of use during equipment repair | $2,500 |

## Deductible

A **$500 deductible** applies per breakdown occurrence, in place of the base all-perils
deductible.

## Settlement basis

Covered equipment less than **10 years old** is settled on a **replacement cost** basis.
Equipment **10 years or older** is settled on an **actual cash value** basis, with
depreciation applied at 5% per year to a floor of 30% of replacement cost.

## Claim handling note

Equipment breakdown claims require the **failed component to be retained** for inspection
until the claim is closed. Disposal of the failed unit before inspection may result in
settlement on an actual cash value basis regardless of age. Claims above **$25,000** are
referred to the Equipment Breakdown specialist unit and require a forensic engineer report.
