# Endorsement HO-2026-04 — Scheduled Personal Property and Replacement Cost on Contents

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** rated per scheduled item; $35 policy fee plus item rates
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## Part 1 — Replacement cost settlement on Coverage C

The base form settles Coverage C personal property on an **actual cash value** basis. When
this endorsement is attached, covered personal property losses are settled on a
**replacement cost** basis, meaning no deduction for depreciation.

Replacement cost settlement is subject to these conditions:

- Meridian Mutual pays **actual cash value first**. The withheld depreciation is paid once
  the property is actually repaired or replaced.
- The claim for the withheld depreciation ("recoverable depreciation") must be made
  **within 180 days** of the date of loss. Extensions of up to 180 further days are granted
  on request where repairs are delayed by contractor availability.
- Replacement cost settlement does **not** apply to antiques, fine art, memorabilia,
  collectibles, or articles whose age or history contributes to their value. These are
  settled on an agreed value basis only if scheduled under Part 2.

## Part 2 — Scheduled personal property

Individually listed items are covered for the **agreed value** shown on the schedule, on an
**all-risk** basis, anywhere in the world. Scheduling an item removes it from the base form
special limits of liability shown in the base policy table.

### Deductible

**No deductible applies** to scheduled items. The base all-perils deductible of $1,000 does
not apply to a loss involving only scheduled property.

### Classes available for scheduling

| Class | Appraisal required above | Automatic newly acquired coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jewellery | $5,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Furs | $5,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Fine art and antiques | $10,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 90 days |
| Silverware and goldware | $10,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Cameras and photographic equipment | $7,500 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Musical instruments | $7,500 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Firearms | $5,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Golf and sporting equipment | $5,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Bicycles and e-bikes | $3,000 per item | 25% of class limit, 30 days |
| Stamp and coin collections | $10,000 per collection | Not available |

**Appraisals** must be dated within **three years** of the policy inception or renewal
date. An appraisal older than three years may be rejected, and the item is then settled at
the lesser of the scheduled amount or the market value at the date of loss.

### Blanket versus itemised

Jewellery may be written on a **blanket** basis up to a total of **$25,000** with no item
appraisal, provided no single item exceeds **$3,000**. Blanket jewellery carries a
per-item payment cap of **$3,000** regardless of the actual value of the item lost.

## Exclusions specific to this endorsement

Scheduled property coverage does not apply to loss caused by:

- **wear and tear**, gradual deterioration, insects or vermin
- **inherent vice** or any repairing, restoring or retouching process
- **mysterious disappearance of unscheduled items** — this remains a Coverage C theft
  question under the base form
- breakage of fragile articles such as glassware or porcelain, **unless** caused by fire,
  theft, vandalism, or the collapse of a building — this breakage exclusion does not apply
  to scientific instruments, cameras or musical instruments
- loss occurring while the property is in the custody of a **transportation carrier** for
  stamp or coin collections

## Claim handling note

Scheduled item claims require the **original appraisal or purchase receipt** on file.
Where no appraisal is on file and the item value exceeds the class appraisal threshold, the
claim is referred to the Valuation unit and cycle time extends to **20 business days**.
Pairs and sets are settled on the **loss-of-value-to-the-set** basis, not by paying the full
set value for the loss of one article.
