# Endorsement HO-2026-05 — Increased Ordinance or Law Coverage

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** $31 annual at the 25% option, $74 annual at the 50% option
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

Exclusion 1 of the base form excludes the increased cost of construction that results from
enforcement of a building code, ordinance or law. This endorsement **increases the
ordinance or law allowance** to the percentage of the Coverage A limit shown in the
Declarations.

Without this endorsement, the base form provides an ordinance or law allowance of
**10% of the Coverage A limit**. With this endorsement, the allowance is increased to
either **25%** or **50%** of the Coverage A limit, as elected.

## Costs covered

The increased allowance applies to the reasonable and necessary increased cost to:

1. **Repair or rebuild** the damaged portion of a covered building to comply with the code
   in force at the time of loss;
2. **Demolish and clear the site** of the undamaged portion of a building, where the code
   requires demolition of the whole structure because a percentage of it was destroyed;
3. Comply with requirements for **accessibility, energy efficiency, fire suppression,
   seismic bracing and wind mitigation** where those requirements are triggered by the
   repair.

The allowance is an **additional amount of insurance** and does not reduce the Coverage A
or Coverage B limits.

## Costs not covered

This endorsement does not pay for:

- costs to comply with a code where the building **was not damaged** by a covered loss;
- costs relating to **pollutants, contaminants or asbestos** removal, except where removal
  is required solely because of a covered loss and is limited to **$10,000**;
- the cost of any code compliance work the insured **elects** to perform but which is not
  required by the authority having jurisdiction;
- fines or penalties for **non-compliance predating the loss**;
- increased costs arising from an **earth movement** or **flood** loss, both of which
  remain excluded under the base form.

## Undamaged portion condition

Where a code requires demolition of an undamaged portion of the dwelling, coverage applies
only if the damaged portion **exceeds 50% of the total floor area** or the authority
having jurisdiction issues a written demolition order. A verbal instruction from an
inspector is not sufficient.

## Time limit

Increased construction costs are payable only if the repair or rebuild is **actually
completed within 24 months** of the date of loss. The company may extend this period in
writing for good cause, including permit delays outside the insured's control.

## Claim handling note

Ordinance or law claims require the **permit set and the written code citation** from the
authority having jurisdiction. Estimators must produce a **split estimate** showing the
like-for-like repair cost separately from the code-upgrade cost, because only the
difference is payable under this endorsement. Claims where the code-upgrade component
exceeds **$50,000** require Large Loss unit approval.
