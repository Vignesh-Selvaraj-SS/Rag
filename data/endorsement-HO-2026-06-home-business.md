# Endorsement HO-2026-06 — Home Business and Home Sharing

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** $96 annual base, plus $40 per additional insured location
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

The base form limits business property to **$3,000** on the residence premises and **$750**
away from it, and excludes business pursuits under Coverage E personal liability. This
endorsement extends both property and liability coverage to a **qualifying home business**
conducted from the residence premises.

## Qualifying home business

A business qualifies if **all** of the following are true:

- annual **gross receipts do not exceed $250,000**;
- the business has **no more than three employees**, including the insured;
- there are **no more than 12 client visits** to the residence premises in any week;
- the business does not involve manufacturing, food preparation for sale, firearms sales,
  child or adult day care, or vehicle repair.

A business that fails any of these tests must be written on a separate commercial policy.
Coverage under this endorsement is **void from inception** for a business that never
qualified.

## Part 1 — Business property

The Coverage C special limit for business property is increased to:

| Location | Increased limit |
| --- | --- |
| On the residence premises | $50,000 |
| Away from the residence premises, worldwide | $10,000 |
| Business property in a vehicle | $5,000 |
| Business data and records restoration | $10,000 |

Business **stock held for sale** is limited to **$15,000** within the on-premises limit.
Coverage does **not** apply to accounts receivable, currency, or property of others held
for repair beyond **$5,000**.

## Part 2 — Business liability

Coverage E is extended to bodily injury and property damage arising from the qualifying
home business, with a limit of **$500,000 per occurrence** and a **$1,000,000 annual
aggregate**.

This does **not** include professional liability. Claims arising from professional advice,
design, accounting, medical, legal or financial services are excluded and require a separate
errors and omissions policy.

## Part 3 — Home sharing

Where the residence premises is rented to short-term paying guests through a hosting
platform, coverage applies subject to:

- rental for no more than **90 days in any policy year**;
- no more than **30 consecutive days** to a single guest;
- theft of personal property by a guest is covered up to **$5,000** with a **$1,000
  deductible**, and requires a police report;
- liability to a paying guest is covered under the **$500,000** Part 2 limit;
- damage caused by a guest to the dwelling is covered under Coverage A, subject to the base
  all-perils deductible.

Coverage does not apply where the dwelling is rented **in whole for more than 90 days**, as
the premises then cease to be a residence premises under the base form.

## Business income

Loss of business income following a covered loss is payable up to **$25,000** for a period
not exceeding **12 months**, based on the net income the business would have earned. Proof
requires the **two most recent filed tax returns** or, for a business under two years old,
year-to-date bookkeeping records certified by the insured.

## Claim handling note

Home business claims require verification of qualifying status **as at the date of loss**,
not the policy inception date. The adjuster must obtain gross receipts and employee count
before reserving. Home sharing claims require the **platform booking record** identifying
the guest and the rental dates.
