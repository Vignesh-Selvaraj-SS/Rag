# Endorsement HO-2026-07 — Identity Fraud and Cyber Event Expense

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** $29 annual
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

Adds expense reimbursement coverage for an insured who is the victim of an **identity fraud
event** or a **personal cyber event**. This is an expense-reimbursement coverage, not a
liability coverage.

## Definitions

**Identity fraud event** means the theft or unauthorised use of an insured's personal
identifying information — name, national insurance or social security number, date of
birth, bank or payment card credentials — to commit fraud.

**Personal cyber event** means malware, ransomware, unauthorised access to an insured's
personal device or online account, or online fraud committed against an insured, occurring
on a device owned by or under the control of an insured at the residence premises.

## Limit of liability

The aggregate limit is **$25,000 per policy year**, regardless of the number of insureds or
events. Within that aggregate:

| Covered expense | Sub-limit |
| --- | --- |
| Legal fees to defend suits and remove fraudulent judgments | $10,000 |
| Lost wages, up to $500 per week | $5,000 |
| Costs of notarising affidavits, certified mail, credit reports | $2,000 |
| Data restoration and device reformatting | $5,000 |
| **Ransomware payment** | **$5,000** — prior written consent required |
| Cyber extortion negotiation and specialist fees | $5,000 |
| Fraudulent online purchase reimbursement | $2,500 |
| Credit monitoring for 12 months following an event | Provided as a service, no cash value |

## Deductible

A **$250 deductible** applies per identity fraud or cyber event.

## Conditions

- The insured must **report the event to the police, the bank, and the affected
  institutions within 30 days** of discovery.
- A **ransomware payment is only reimbursed** where Meridian Mutual gave **prior written
  consent** and where paying was the only reasonable means of recovering the data.
- Devices must have had **commercially available anti-malware software installed and
  current** at the time of the event, for the cyber portion only. This condition does not
  apply to the identity fraud portion.
- Coverage applies to events **first discovered during the policy period**, even where the
  underlying fraud began before inception, provided the policy has been continuously in
  force for at least **12 months**.

## Exclusions specific to this endorsement

This endorsement does not cover:

- **the stolen funds themselves** — only the expenses of recovery. Money taken from a bank
  account is recoverable from the financial institution, not under this policy;
- losses arising from a **business** conducted by an insured, including a home business
  covered under endorsement HO-2026-06;
- fraud committed by a **family member or a person residing in the household**;
- **investment losses**, including cryptocurrency, whether or not obtained by fraud;
- loss arising from the insured **knowingly transferring funds** to a party later found to
  be fraudulent, where the transfer followed a request the insured did not verify — except
  that up to **$2,500** is payable under the fraudulent online purchase sub-limit.

## Claim handling note

Identity fraud claims are handled by the **Specialty Lines desk**, not the property team.
First contact target is **two business days**. A completed **Identity Theft Affidavit** and
the police report reference number are required before any expense is reimbursed.
Ransomware claims must be escalated to the Cyber duty manager **before** any payment is
discussed with the insured.
