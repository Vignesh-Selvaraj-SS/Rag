# Endorsement HO-2026-08 — Roof Surfaces Actual Cash Value and Wind/Hail Schedule

**Attaches to:** HO-3-2026 base form
**Effective:** 01 March 2026
**Premium:** credit of $145 to $410 annually depending on roof age and territory
**Endorsement pack:** 2026 Spring Release

---

## What this endorsement does

This is a **coverage-restricting endorsement** applied in exchange for a premium credit. It
changes the loss settlement basis for **roof surfacing** from replacement cost to
**actual cash value** where the loss is caused by **windstorm or hail**.

All other causes of loss to the roof — fire, falling objects, weight of ice and snow,
vandalism, aircraft, vehicles, explosion — continue to be settled on the base form
replacement cost basis. The restriction applies **only** to wind and hail.

## Definition of roof surfacing

**Roof surfacing** means the shingles, tiles, cladding, metal panels or other materials
forming the outermost weatherproofing layer of the roof, together with the underlayment,
flashing, vents, gutters and downspouts attached to it.

It does **not** include the roof decking, trusses, rafters or interior ceilings, which
continue to be settled on a replacement cost basis under Coverage A.

## Depreciation schedule

Actual cash value is calculated as replacement cost less depreciation, applied by roof age
and material as follows:

| Roof material | Expected life | Annual depreciation | Maximum depreciation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Composition / asphalt shingle (3-tab) | 20 years | 5.0% per year | 80% |
| Architectural / laminated shingle | 30 years | 3.3% per year | 70% |
| Wood shake or shingle | 25 years | 4.0% per year | 75% |
| Metal panel or standing seam | 40 years | 2.5% per year | 60% |
| Clay or concrete tile | 50 years | 2.0% per year | 50% |
| Slate | 60 years | 1.7% per year | 50% |
| Modified bitumen / built-up flat roof | 20 years | 5.0% per year | 80% |

Roof age is measured from the **date the roof surfacing was last fully replaced**, as
evidenced by a permit, a contractor invoice, or the underwriting inspection photograph on
file. Where the roof age **cannot be established**, the age of the dwelling is used.

## Cosmetic damage

**Cosmetic damage** to metal roof surfacing — meaning dents, dimples or marring caused by
hail which do not diminish the roof's ability to shed water — is **excluded entirely**, not
merely depreciated. A metal roof with cosmetic hail dimpling and no penetration is not a
covered loss under this endorsement.

## Interaction with the windstorm deductible

Where this endorsement applies, the **2% windstorm or hail deductible** from the base form
is applied **first**, to the replacement cost figure, and depreciation is then applied to
the remainder. Applying depreciation before the deductible is incorrect and is a common
reopen reason.

## When this endorsement may not be applied

This endorsement may not be attached where:

- the roof surfacing is **less than 5 years old** at policy inception;
- the dwelling is in a territory where the regulator prohibits roof ACV settlement —
  currently territories **41, 42 and 47**;
- the policy is written on the HO-8 modified form.

## Claim handling note

Roof claims under this endorsement require **date-stamped aerial or drone imagery** and a
test square of at least **10 feet by 10 feet** documenting hail impact density. Adjusters
must record the roof material and age in the claim file **before** producing the estimate,
because the depreciation rate cannot be applied retrospectively without a re-inspection.
Disputes over roof age account for the largest share of reopened wind and hail claims.
