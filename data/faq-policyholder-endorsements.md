# Policyholder FAQ — 2026 Spring Endorsement Pack

**Published:** 01 March 2026
**Audience:** Policyholders and contact-centre staff
**Source of truth:** Where this FAQ and a policy form disagree, the **policy form governs**.

---

### My basement flooded after heavy rain. Am I covered?

It depends on how the water got in. If water **backed up through a drain or sewer**, or a
**sump pump overflowed**, and you have endorsement HO-2026-01, you are covered up to
$10,000 (or $25,000 if you chose the higher limit) with a $500 deductible. If **surface
water flowed in** from outside — over the ground, through a window well, or from a river or
storm surge — that is **flood**, and flood is not covered by any endorsement in this pack.
Flood cover must be bought separately.

### The pipe from my house to the street broke. Who pays?

If the break is on **your side** of the utility connection point and you have endorsement
HO-2026-02, we pay up to $15,000 including excavation and reinstating your lawn or
driveway, with a $500 deductible. If the break is on the **utility's side**, the utility is
responsible and we would decline and refer it to them.

### My air conditioner died. Is that a claim?

Only if it **broke down suddenly**. Endorsement HO-2026-03 covers sudden mechanical or
electrical breakdown up to $100,000 with a $500 deductible. It does not cover an old unit
that simply wore out, and it does not cover anything a manufacturer's warranty still
covers. If the unit is 10 years or older we settle at actual cash value, not replacement
cost.

### My freezer failed and I lost all the food.

Food spoilage is covered up to **$1,000** under endorsement HO-2026-03, but only where the
spoilage followed a covered **breakdown** of the freezer or of the electrical supply
equipment. Spoilage from an off-premises power cut with no equipment damage is not covered,
because power failure away from the premises is excluded by the base form.

### How much jewellery is covered without listing it?

Under the base policy, jewellery is covered for **$2,000 for theft** — that is a hard
limit no matter how much the piece is worth. To cover more, schedule each item under
endorsement HO-2026-04. Scheduled items have **no deductible** and are covered worldwide
against almost any cause of loss, including simply losing them.

### Do I need an appraisal?

For jewellery and furs, yes, above **$5,000 per item**. For fine art, antiques, silverware
and coin collections, above **$10,000**. The appraisal must be **less than three years
old** at inception or renewal. You can insure jewellery on a blanket basis up to $25,000
with no appraisal, but no single item is then paid above $3,000.

### My roof was damaged by hail. Why is the cheque less than the estimate?

If endorsement HO-2026-08 is on your policy — which you would have accepted in exchange for
a premium credit — the roof surfacing is settled at **actual cash value** for wind and hail
losses. We take the replacement cost, subtract your windstorm deductible, then apply
depreciation based on the roof's age and material. A 12-year-old asphalt shingle roof
depreciates at 5% a year, so about 60% is deducted.

Damage to the **decking, ceilings and interior** is still paid at full replacement cost.
Only the outer surfacing is depreciated.

### My metal roof is dented but not leaking.

Under endorsement HO-2026-08, **cosmetic damage to metal roofing is excluded outright**.
Dents that do not stop the roof shedding water are not a covered loss.

### Someone opened a credit card in my name.

Endorsement HO-2026-07 reimburses the **costs of putting it right** — legal fees, lost
wages up to $500 a week, notarising and postage, credit monitoring — up to $25,000 a year
with a $250 deductible. It does **not** repay the stolen money itself; your bank or card
issuer is responsible for that.

### I run a small business from home. Is my equipment covered?

Only $3,000 of business property on the premises under the base policy. Endorsement
HO-2026-06 raises that to $50,000 and adds $500,000 of business liability, provided your
business takes under $250,000 a year, has three or fewer people, and sees no more than 12
client visits a week. Professional advice claims are never covered — that needs a separate
errors and omissions policy.

### I rent my house out on a hosting site sometimes.

Covered under endorsement HO-2026-06 for up to **90 days a year** and no more than 30
consecutive days per guest. Theft by a guest is covered to $5,000 with a $1,000 deductible
and needs a police report.

### How quickly will someone contact me?

If your home is unliveable, there is an injury, or the loss is over $100,000, we aim to
contact you within **4 hours**. Significant but liveable damage: **1 business day**.
Standard claims: **2 business days**. You get a claim number within 30 minutes of
reporting, whatever the channel.

### Can the person who takes my call tell me if I'm covered?

No. Intake staff issue the claim number and arrange emergency help, but the coverage
decision is made by an adjuster after reviewing your policy. They can authorise emergency
work such as water extraction or boarding up to **$5,000** straight away, and advance up to
$3,000 of living expenses if your home is uninhabitable.

### How long do I have to claim depreciation back?

Where replacement cost applies, we pay actual cash value first and hold back the
depreciation. Once you have actually repaired or replaced the item, claim the held-back
amount **within 180 days** of the loss. Ask us and we will normally extend that by another
180 days if contractors are slow.
