# Homeowners Policy HO-3 — Base Form (2026 Edition)

**Form number:** HO-3-2026
**Effective:** 01 January 2026
**Underwriter:** Meridian Mutual Insurance Company
**Applies to:** All new and renewal homeowners policies written on or after the effective date.

---

## Section I — Property Coverages

### Coverage A — Dwelling

Covers the dwelling on the residence premises shown in the Declarations, including
structures attached to the dwelling. Materials and supplies located on or next to the
residence premises used to construct, alter or repair the dwelling are also covered.

The Coverage A limit is shown in the Declarations. Coverage A is written on a
**replacement cost** basis provided the dwelling is insured to at least **80% of its full
replacement cost** at the time of loss. If the dwelling is insured for less than 80%, losses
are settled on an **actual cash value** basis.

### Coverage B — Other Structures

Covers structures on the residence premises separated from the dwelling by clear space,
such as detached garages, sheds, fences and in-ground pools. The Coverage B limit is
**10% of the Coverage A limit** unless a higher limit is shown in the Declarations. This
is an additional amount of insurance and does not reduce Coverage A.

Coverage B does not apply to any structure rented to a person who is not a tenant of the
dwelling, or to any structure from which a business is conducted.

### Coverage C — Personal Property

Covers personal property owned or used by an insured, anywhere in the world. The
Coverage C limit is **50% of the Coverage A limit** unless a higher limit is shown in the
Declarations. Personal property is settled on an **actual cash value** basis under the base
form; replacement cost settlement requires endorsement HO-2026-04.

**Special limits of liability.** These limits do not increase the Coverage C limit. The
following sub-limits apply per occurrence:

| Category | Special limit |
| --- | --- |
| Money, bank notes, bullion, coins | $250 |
| Securities, deeds, manuscripts, tickets | $1,750 |
| Watercraft including trailers and equipment | $1,750 |
| Trailers not used with watercraft | $1,750 |
| Jewelry, watches, furs — theft only | $2,000 |
| Firearms and related equipment — theft only | $2,750 |
| Silverware and goldware — theft only | $2,750 |
| Business property on the residence premises | $3,000 |
| Business property away from the residence premises | $750 |
| Portable electronic equipment in a motor vehicle | $1,750 |

### Coverage D — Loss of Use

Provides Additional Living Expense and Fair Rental Value when a covered loss makes the
residence premises unfit to live in. The Coverage D limit is **30% of the Coverage A
limit**. Payment continues for the shortest time reasonably required to repair or replace
the damage, and is **capped at 24 months** regardless of policy expiration.

### Coverage E — Personal Liability

Pays damages an insured is legally liable for because of bodily injury or property damage
caused by an occurrence. The base limit is **$300,000 per occurrence**. Defence costs are
paid in addition to the limit of liability.

### Coverage F — Medical Payments to Others

Pays necessary medical expenses incurred within **three years** of an accident causing
bodily injury. The base limit is **$5,000 per person**. Coverage F does not apply to an
insured or to a regular resident of the residence premises other than a residence employee.

---

## Deductibles

The base all-perils deductible is **$1,000 per occurrence**, applied to Section I property
losses only. No deductible applies to Coverage E or Coverage F.

A separate **windstorm or hail deductible of 2% of the Coverage A limit** applies in
designated coastal territories, as shown in the Declarations. Where both the all-perils and
the windstorm deductible could apply to a single occurrence, only the **larger** of the two
is deducted.

---

## Section I — Perils Insured Against

Coverage A and Coverage B are insured against **risk of direct physical loss** to
property, except as excluded. Coverage C is insured against the following **named
perils** only: fire or lightning; windstorm or hail; explosion; riot or civil commotion;
aircraft; vehicles; smoke; vandalism or malicious mischief; theft; falling objects; weight
of ice, snow or sleet; accidental discharge or overflow of water or steam; sudden and
accidental tearing apart of a heating or air conditioning system; freezing; and sudden and
accidental damage from artificially generated electrical current.

---

## Section I — Exclusions

The following are excluded under the base form. Some may be added back by endorsement.

1. **Ordinance or law** — increased costs from enforcement of any building code.
   *May be added back by endorsement HO-2026-05.*
2. **Earth movement** — earthquake, landslide, sinkhole, mudflow, subsidence.
3. **Water damage** — flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of a body of
   water, and **water which backs up through sewers or drains**.
   *Sewer and drain backup may be added back by endorsement HO-2026-01.*
4. **Power failure** occurring off the residence premises.
5. **Neglect** of an insured to use reasonable means to save and preserve property.
6. **War**, including undeclared war and civil war.
7. **Nuclear hazard.**
8. **Intentional loss** by or at the direction of an insured.
9. **Governmental action** — seizure or destruction by order of a public authority.
10. **Wear and tear**, marring, deterioration, mechanical breakdown, latent defect,
    rust, mould, wet or dry rot, and smog.
    *Mechanical and electrical breakdown may be added back by endorsement HO-2026-03.*

## Duties After Loss

In the event of a loss the insured must:

1. Give **prompt notice** to Meridian Mutual or its agent.
2. Notify the police when the loss involves theft.
3. Protect the property from further damage and make reasonable emergency repairs,
   keeping records of the expense.
4. Prepare an inventory of damaged personal property showing quantity, description,
   actual cash value and amount of loss.
5. Submit a signed, sworn **proof of loss within 60 days** of the company's request.
6. Cooperate in the investigation, including examination under oath if requested.

A claim may be denied where late notice materially prejudices the company's ability to
investigate.

## Suit Against Us

No action may be brought against Meridian Mutual unless the policy provisions have been
complied with and the action is started **within two years** after the date of loss.
